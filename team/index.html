Shawn Long

Shawn Long is an accomplished technology executive, entrepreneur, and cybersecurity leader with more than three decades of experience spanning healthcare technology, regulatory compliance, data protection, and enterprise risk management.

Since 1996, the former CEO of MedcomSoft a healthcare technology company. Shawn has gained extensive experience navigating the complex requirements surrounding HIPAA, patient data privacy, regulatory compliance, data integrity, and the secure management of sensitive healthcare information. His leadership experience in the highly regulated healthcare sector provided him with a deep understanding of the operational, legal, and reputational risks associated with the collection, storage, use, and protection of confidential data.

In 2008, Mr. Long founded viLogics, an information technology and cybersecurity services company specializing in helping organizations protect their critical systems, sensitive information, and intellectual property. Through his leadership at viLogics, he has developed significant expertise in cybersecurity governance, risk management, compliance, data-loss prevention, incident response, infrastructure security, and the implementation of practical security frameworks across complex organizations.

As artificial intelligence rapidly changes how businesses create, process, analyze, and share information, Mr. Long has expanded his focus to include AI governance, regulatory compliance, and the emerging risks associated with employee and organizational use of public large language models. He brings particular insight into the risks of confidential data, customer information, proprietary business processes, intellectual property, and regulated information being unintentionally transmitted—or egressed—into public AI platforms.

As an Executive Advisor, Mr. Long brings a direct, no-nonsense approach to helping organizations establish disciplined and enforceable AI policies. His work focuses on creating practical governance frameworks that address responsible AI use, data classification, access controls, acceptable-use standards, regulatory obligations, intellectual property protection, data-loss prevention, vendor risk, employee education, and executive accountability.

Mr. Long’s ability to bridge technology, cybersecurity, compliance, and business operations enables organizations to adopt artificial intelligence responsibly without exposing themselves to unnecessary legal, financial, operational, or reputational risk. His objective is straightforward: help companies benefit from AI while maintaining control of their data, protecting their intellectual property, and operating within a structured, auditable, and compliant framework.

Dr. Gerald Zahorchak

Dr. Gerald L. Zahorchak is a nationally recognized education leader, former Pennsylvania Secretary of Education, and longtime public-school administrator whose career has focused on expanding opportunity and improving student achievement. His experience spans elementary, secondary, higher education, and state-level leadership.

Dr. Zahorchak began his career in Pennsylvania public schools, serving as a teacher, football coach, principal, federal programs director, strategic planning coordinator, and personnel director. He held leadership roles in the North Star, Shanksville-Stonycreek, and Greater Johnstown school districts, ultimately serving as superintendent of the Greater Johnstown School District from 1997 to 2003. He later served as superintendent of the Allentown School District and returned to lead the Johnstown School District. He also taught as an Associate Professor of Education and chaired the Education Division at the University of Pittsburgh at Johnstown.

In 2003, Dr. Zahorchak was appointed Pennsylvania’s Deputy Secretary for Elementary and Secondary Education. In that role, he launched Project 720 and Classrooms for the Future, nationally recognized high school reform initiatives. Governor Edward G. Rendell appointed him Pennsylvania Secretary of Education in 2005, a position he held through May 2010. As the Commonwealth’s chief state school officer, he oversaw education for nearly two million students and administered more than $9 billion annually in education resources.

During his tenure as secretary, Dr. Zahorchak advanced major statewide initiatives, including funding for pre-kindergarten and full-day kindergarten, a new school funding formula, increased support for school districts, the Keystone Exams, and Pennsylvania’s Standards Aligned System. He also helped establish the Pennsylvania Inspired Leadership Program, a statewide professional-learning initiative for school leaders.

His work emphasized Systems Thinking highlighted by standards-based education, effective teaching, strong leadership, and collaborative reform involving educators, administrators, and community partners. Under his leadership, Pennsylvania was the only state in the nation to be recognized for improving student achievement for all students and all student students subgroup populations as tested on the national and state assessments for eight consecutive years.

Dr. Zahorchak earned a Bachelor of Science degree from Saint Francis College, a Master of Education degree from Indiana University of Pennsylvania, and a doctorate in Education Administration from Penn State University. He also received honorary Doctor of Laws degrees from the Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine and Indiana University of Pennsylvania.

Beyond education, Dr. Zahorchak has served his community as deputy mayor of Johnstown, PA, an eight-year member of Johnstown City Council, and a member of the Greater Johnstown School Board. He has been widely published, speaks frequently at education conferences and forums, and has served on numerous state and national boards dedicated to strengthening public and higher education.
